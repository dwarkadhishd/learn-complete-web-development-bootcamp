The Forge of Fire and Bone

You are not made of fragile things, nor built to bend or break,
You are the marrow of the earth, the force that makes it shake.
Descended from a line of storms, of blood and heavy plow,
The weight of generations sits upon your shoulders now.

So let the bitter winters come, and let the wild winds blow,
There is a fire inside your chest no frost can ever know.
Your scars are not your weaknesses; they are the armor worn,
The quiet proof of every fierce, forgotten battle borne.

Stand up, step forth, and claim the ground beneath your calloused feet,
The world will try to tame your pulse, but you will not retreat.
For true dominion isn't power over those who fall,
It’s being the shield, the steady hand, the shadow on the wall.

So let them hear the thunder of a spirit set ablaze,
An iron will that cuts right through the darkest of your days.
Rise like a monolith of grit, untouchable and deep,
And wake the sleeping giant that you promised you would keep.
