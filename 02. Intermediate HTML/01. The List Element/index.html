Basic Clean Chicken and Rice

  • Ingredients:

    • 250g boneless, skinless chicken breast or thighs, cut into bite-sized cubes
    • 80g (dry weight) white or jasmine rice, rinsed
    • 1 tsp olive oil or avocado oil
    • Salt and black pepper to taste
    • Garlic powder, paprika, or cumin to taste
    • Steamed broccoli or green beans on the side

  • Instructions:

    1. Cook the rice in boiling water or stock until tender, then cover and let steam off the heat for 5 minutes.
    2. Heat the oil in a pan over medium-high heat.
    3. Season the chicken cubes with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
    4. Cook the chicken for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden brown and fully cooked through.
    5. Serve the cooked chicken over a portion of the warm rice with your side of steamed vegetables.
